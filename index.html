About Aalia

Five years of speech and language therapy, one person at a time.

Aalia Naeem is a compassionate Speech and Language Pathologist with experience assessing and treating children and adults with autism spectrum disorder, speech delay, language disorders, fluency issues, and hearing-related communication difficulties.

She develops individualized therapy plans, conducts detailed assessments, and collaborates closely with parents and multidisciplinary teams to improve functional communication outcomes.

She currently practices at Noor Surgical Hospital, Haripur, with a background spanning school-based therapy and clinical internships across Haripur and Islamabad.
